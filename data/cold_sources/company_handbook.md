# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (excerpt)

## Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per year, credited monthly. PTO
carries over up to a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year.

## Expense Reimbursement
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date through the
Expensify portal, with a receipt attached for any amount over $25.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require written approval from both the manager and HR business partner.
